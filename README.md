### C+2: Keep C++ Great

**This is not goodbye. This is a passing of the torch.**

They say C++ is over—too dangerous, too flawed. That Rust is the future.

**We disagree.**

The core author of C+2 has been a C++ programmer since his college years—and it has been his language for life. He wrote everything in C++ —— online games, arcade games, set-top box firmware, a Chinese input method, a digital map engine, stock trading software, an AI Go program, and even a large language model.

Then AI took off, and everyone turned to Python. Then memory safety became a priority, and everyone turned to Rust.

**But he refused to abandon C++. Refused to let the language he had poured half his life into be cast aside.**

Because he knows that **C++ is Thor's hammer (Mjölnir)**—only the strongest hands can lift it and strike with the force of thunder.

C++ is also **the sword in the stone (Excalibur)**—only the truest kings can draw it and cut through the hardest of problems.

**If you write great code in C++, you are among the finest warriors anywhere.** You wield the most complex, the sharpest, the most unforgiving tools. You built the digital foundations of human civilization—operating systems, browsers, game engines, financial systems, and flight control for spacecraft.

They say PHP is the best language in the world. But if it falls short at anything, you just write a module in C—because ultimately, everything other languages cannot do falls to C and C++.

Python slow? NumPy is written in C. JavaScript needs more performance? V8 is C++. AI frameworks? PyTorch's core is C++. The JVM itself? C++. Database engines? C++. Game engines? C++. Operating system kernels? C and C++.

**C and C++ programmers have always been the bedrock of the entire digital world.**

But greatness is not perfection. C++ has one fatal flaw: **memory safety relies entirely on human vigilance.** Even the finest warriors stumble.

**That era is over.**

Today, we put a shield on this legendary blade.

## What is C+2?

**C+2 is the direct successor to C++. Not a fork, not a variant—an evolution.**

> **C+2 = C++ control at the metal + Rust safety at compile time + Python flexibility at the high level + TypeScript reach to the frontend**

We incorporated the most valuable idea from Rust—ownership and borrow checking. But we discarded none of C++'s legacy. Template metaprogramming, RAII, operator overloading, STL—**everything you know and love stays.**

We absorbed Python's expressiveness in AI—tensors, neural networks, automatic differentiation—all in clean, concise syntax, with zero performance cost.

**C+2 compiles to TypeScript, JavaScript, and WebAssembly**—taking your code from the operating system kernel to every pixel on a browser screen.

And most importantly: **gradual safety.**

Enable safety mode on your most critical modules first, then extend it across your codebase incrementally. **No need to rewrite everything overnight.**

**Keep C++ Great means embracing the future without abandoning the past.**

---

## Why not just use Rust?

Rust is excellent. **But it is not C++.**

C++ has its own unique elegance—the freedom to control every pixel, every audio pulse, and to define neural networks with the same concise syntax as Python. From hardware registers to browser pixels, C++ has always been the most liberating tool across every layer of the stack. And C+2 extends that freedom to every corner of the Web.

**C+2 does not take that freedom away. It simply puts guardrails around it.**

The sword in the stone (Excalibur) is still the sword in the stone. Only now, **it never dulls.**

**The story of C+2's author is all of our stories.** We don't have to abandon C++ to keep it great. We only need to give it a soul of safety.
